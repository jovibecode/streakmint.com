Support

A rule shows Unverified or isn't updating

Verified rules read Apple Health. Check that StreakMint can read your data in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → StreakMint. In the app, tap a Verified rule to see where its data comes from.

Restore a purchase

Open StreakMint → Settings (gear on Today) → Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchase on the first screen.

Cancel a subscription

Settings on your iPhone → [your name] → Subscriptions → StreakMint.
